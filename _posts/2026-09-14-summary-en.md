### [Trump Rejects Tech Executives&\#x27; Calls to Slow AI Development](https://www.ft.com/content/cae60732-f929-4735-a627-db8c14e7c7ed?syn-25a6b1a6=1) ⭐️ 6.0/10

US President Donald Trump rejected calls from technology executives to slow the pace of artificial intelligence development and opposed tightening regulation on safety-risk grounds, according to a brief Financial Times report. Facing pressure from parts of the tech industry and from Democrats for stricter rules, Trump attributed the concerns to negative forces and stressed that the United States must not fall behind China in the AI race. The account frames the stance as a signal that the current US administration will prioritize competitive speed over safety-based restrictions. The supplied report names no specific executives, dates, policy measures, or regulatory proposals, so what the stance means in practice for AI developers, regulators, or the public remains unclear.

Trump&\#x27;s remarks came during a visit to Ireland, and reporting indicates Democrats have seized on his administration&\#x27;s resistance to regulation as a political line of attack. The debate is framed largely around AI capability and national-security competition with China, with House Speaker Mike Johnson also emphasizing the need to maintain an edge over China while balancing AI safety concerns.
