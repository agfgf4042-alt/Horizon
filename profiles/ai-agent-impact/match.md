# AI agents and human impact profile

Use this profile for substantive developments about AI agents and their effects on people at any level: individuals, families, workers, consumers, students, patients, creators, communities, organizations, institutions, and society.

Include agentic systems, autonomous workflows, multi-agent systems, deployment incidents, evaluations, labor and livelihood changes, education, health, mental well-being, relationships, accessibility, privacy, surveillance, discrimination, misinformation, security, safety, law, regulation, governance, and accountability when the item has a concrete connection to human consequences.

Do not use this profile for routine model releases, benchmark gains, coding tips, infrastructure-only updates, product marketing, or generic AI commentary unless the item provides clear evidence about how people may be affected.
