# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the importance of developments concerning AI agents and their concrete effects on people, organizations, institutions, and public life.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Groundbreaking.** Strong evidence of a major shift in how agents affect human safety, rights, livelihoods, health, education, relationships, institutions, or accountability; major incidents, policy decisions, or rigorous research with broad consequences.
- **7-8: High value.** Clear and well-supported evidence about agent capabilities in real settings, meaningful benefits or harms to people, governance responses, or adoption with implications beyond one product.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Relevant case study, evaluation, debate, or early signal with useful evidence but limited scope or uncertain consequences.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Speculative commentary, routine announcements, weakly evidenced claims, or mostly technical material with only an incidental human angle.
- **0-2: Noise.** Promotional, repetitive, off-topic, unsupported, or purely technical content without a substantive consequence for people.

# Evaluation guidance

Prioritize evidence over hype. Identify who is affected, the mechanism of impact, scale, observed outcomes, uncertainty, and who is accountable. Consider both individual and collective effects. Distinguish demonstrated agent behavior from vendor claims and forecasts. Give extra weight to independent research, documented incidents, policy or legal changes, and credible field evidence. Use three to five specific topic tags.
